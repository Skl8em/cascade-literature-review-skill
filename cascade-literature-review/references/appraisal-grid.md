# Appraisal grid

Four axes.
Axes 1 and 4 are coded per paper; axes 2 and 3 per research programme, with papers inheriting unless overridden with a stated reason.

Coding is a reasoning gesture, not a checklist lookup.
The indicators below are illustrative — the forms these problems most often take, not an exhaustive typology.
When a paper resembles no indicator, that is not evidence it passes; work from the definition.

## 1. Axis 1 — normative structure

**Codes:** `descriptive` | `normative-explicit` | `normative-tacit` | `indeterminate`

The question is not whether a value premise is present — almost every paper about human arrangements rests on one.
It is whether the premise is *named* and presented as *contingent*.

**Procedure.**
Restate the central claim as a conditional: *if premise P, then conclusion Q*.
Isolating P makes the P→Q relation testable exactly as a descriptive claim is, which is why explicit normativity is not a defect.
Then test P:

**(a) Explicitness.**
Is P stated, or does it arrive as shared common sense?
A premise can be present in the prose and still fail, if asserted as background rather than claimed.
Watch above all for premises living in the **operationalisation**: the objective function, the welfare measure, what counts as a cost, what enters the model and what is left outside as externality, which outcome is coded as success.
A paper can be scrupulously neutral in its prose and carry its entire normative load in an equation or a coding scheme.
This is how careful authors fail (a).

**(b) Contingency.**
Is P one option among alternatives a reasonable person could hold, or the only serious position?
A premise named but treated as beyond dispute is not clarified, it is fortified.
Signals: alternatives raised only to be dismissed in a sentence; the literature holding the alternative cited only through its critics; the premise attributed to nobody in particular.

Failing (a) or (b) is `normative-tacit`.
Passing both is `normative-explicit`.
No value premise in the central claim — rarer than it looks, most plausible for measurement and typology work — is `descriptive`.
`indeterminate` where the claim cannot be reconstructed as a conditional from the available text: code it and say why rather than guessing.

**Provenance of the premise.**
When a paper codes `normative-tacit`, run its backward cascade far enough to find where P entered.
Frequently P was argued explicitly, once, decades ago, under conditions since abandoned, and inherited as furniture ever since.
That lineage is worth more to the review than the code, because it turns an accusation into a traceable claim.

## 2. Axis 2 — programme dynamics

**Codes:** `progressive` | `degenerating` | `indeterminate`.
Once per programme.

Does the community carrying this theory have a functioning correction mechanism?
Lakatos's frame: a hard core protected by a belt of auxiliary hypotheses, and what matters is what happens to the belt when anomalies arrive.

**Progressive indicators.**
The core is narrowed, conditioned, made more precise in response to anomalies.
The programme predicts facts it was not built to explain, and some survive testing.
Scope conditions get stated — *this holds for these actors under these institutions*.
Critical results are answered on their own terms: replicated, bounded, or conceded.

**Degenerating indicators.**
Auxiliary assumptions accumulate, each fitted to one anomaly and predicting nothing else.
Disconfirmations are absorbed as measurement problems or as the world failing to satisfy the model's conditions.
The core survives untouched across decades of contrary results.
Critical work is cited perfunctorily or not at all — check whether the strongest published criticisms appear in the citing sets of the programme's own papers, since a programme that does not cite its critics is not answering them.

**Evidence is the forward-channel stance distribution.**
The ratio of `extending` to `rescuing`, and the fate of the `critical-empirical` set, are the operational measure.
Trace two or three anomalies through the citing literature and see what was done with each — more informative than any aggregate.

**Constraints.**
A programme needs time and real empirical confrontation before judgement.
Younger than roughly a decade, or not yet having met a serious anomaly: `indeterminate`, not `progressive` for lack of failures.
Symmetrically, `indeterminate` is the honest code when the corpus is too thin to see the community's behaviour, and it must not drift toward `degenerating` because the reviewer finds the theory uncongenial.

A degenerating programme is not thereby false, and its observations may be sound.
The verdict bears on the warrant its conclusions carry.

## 3. Axis 3 — performative contamination

**Codes:** `contaminated` | `independent` | `indeterminate`.
Per programme × field.

Has the theory, by circulating, shaped the behaviour of the actors it claims to describe?
Where it has, post-diffusion empirical confirmation cannot be read as an independently occurring regularity: the correct reading is that the theory structured the practice.
Merton's self-fulfilling prophecy is the general form; MacKenzie's account of finance theory reshaping the markets it modelled is the developed case, distinguishing *Barnesian performativity* — use of the theory makes the world more like its model — from *counterperformativity*, where use makes it less so.

**Two conditions, both requiring evidence:**

- **A diffusion channel.**
  The actors were exposed: professional training, textbooks, certification syllabi, regulation and accounting standards embedding the model, software and instruments implementing it, consultancy practice, management doctrine.
  The `practitioner-uptake` stance code is the direct evidence.
- **A feedback path into the measured quantity.**
  Exposure alone is not contamination.
  There must be a route by which changed behaviour affects the variable the theory is tested on.
  A theory taught to everyone but tested on something the actors cannot influence is `independent`.

**Direction matters.**
Feedback that makes the model more accurate renders subsequent confirmations near-worthless as tests; feedback working against it compromises disconfirmations equally.
Both are contamination — record which.

**Date the diffusion.**
Contamination has a start.
Empirical work on the field before diffusion is uncontaminated evidence and is often the most valuable material in the corpus.
Flag it in the ledger; this is why the time distribution of the forward set is worth plotting.

The contrast between a population that has not read the theory describing it and one trained on it and evaluated against it is illustrative only.
Domains vary continuously, and the code rests on the two evidenced conditions, not on a discipline's reputation.

## 4. Axis 4 — claim, mode, and purchase

Three orthogonal dimensions, **not a hierarchy of evidence**.
Any single scale encodes a ranking of methods decided before looking, which is the thing to avoid: it smuggles in a view about what counts as knowledge under the appearance of a quality measure.

### (a) Claim type — what is being asserted

`existence` (the phenomenon occurs) · `magnitude` (how much, in a specified population) · `mechanism-discrimination` (which of several rival mechanisms operates) · `process-reconstruction` (how this unfolded, in a case or a sequence) · `meaning-elucidation` (what this is for the actors, in their own categories) · `conceptual-requalification` (the categories in use are badly cut and here are better ones) · `normative-conditional` (if P then Q).

### (b) Evidence mode — how it was produced

`randomised-intervention` · `natural-experiment` · `systematic-observation` · `theoretical-case-selection` · `ethnographic-immersion` · `textual-archival-corpus` · `formal-derivation-only` · `second-order-synthesis` · `none`.

### (c) Purchase — what grip (b) has on (a)

`strong` | `partial` | `weak` | `none`.
This is a **relation**, judged case by case, and it is the whole point of the axis.

A randomised design has strong purchase on `magnitude` in the trial population and close to none on `mechanism-discrimination` or `conceptual-requalification`: it establishes that an effect occurred there, saying nothing about the theory attached to it or about transport elsewhere.
This limitation is well established in the methodological literature [C — see Deaton & Cartwright on the limits of RCTs, *Social Science & Medicine*, 2018; Cartwright & Hardie, *Evidence-Based Policy*, 2012 — verify before citing].

A well-conducted ethnography has strong purchase on `meaning-elucidation` and `conceptual-requalification`, weak purchase on `magnitude`.
And a successful requalification retroactively unsettles what a trial was measuring, which is why the two cannot be ranked against each other: they answer different questions and one can undermine the other's object.

Formal derivation alone has purchase on internal consistency and on what follows from stated assumptions — real, and not a substitute for any empirical claim.

**Two named failure modes, symmetric:**

- `theoretical-inertia` — impeccable identification, no purchase whatever on the theory the paper invokes.
  The design is sound and the conclusion is decoration.
- `overreach` — rich elucidation presented as generalisable magnitude, or a case reconstruction presented as a mechanism established in general.

### Mode-internal quality criteria

Quality is judged inside the mode, not imported from another.
Illustratively: for qualitative and ethnographic work, reflexivity about the investigator's position, systematic treatment of negative cases, categories derived from the field rather than imposed, accessibility of the material, adequate immersion.
For archival and textual corpora, the constitution of the corpus and what it excludes.
For formal work, what the model rules out by construction.
For observational quantitative work, the sampling frame and identification assumptions.

### Population of evidence versus population of conclusion

Record both.
A result rigorous on its sample and silently universalised is methodologically sound and substantively false for everyone outside it — a failure invisible to any check performed inside the paper's own frame.
This applies to every mode, including ethnography.

## 5. Meta-analyses and second-order work

Code the mode as `second-order-synthesis`.
Two specific things:

**Quality is inherited from the primary corpus plus the aggregation assumptions.**
Comparability of estimands, handling of publication bias, whether heterogeneity is modelled or averaged away.
A meta-analysis over incomparable estimands is precise about nothing.

**Axes 2 and 3 are inherited from the programme of the primary studies, not from the meta-analysis itself.**
In a contaminated field a meta-analysis aggregates contamination with a tightened confidence interval — worse than a single study, because the tightening reads as robustness.
Say so explicitly when it applies.

Systematic reviews and handbook chapters are also second-order and get the same treatment; they are additionally useful as non-citational discovery entry points.

## 6. How the axes interact

Axes 2 and 3 are largely independent of axis 1. An openly normative theory can be a progressive programme on an uncontaminated field; a theory descriptive in form can be degenerating and circular.
Do not let a verdict on one axis propagate to the others by association — that is how a grid becomes a way of restating a prior judgement in four columns.

The combination identifying the target failure — a theory imposed on a field, insulated from correction, confirmed by a practice it created — is `normative-tacit` + `degenerating` + `contaminated`.
It is a strong criterion precisely because it requires three independent findings to coincide.

Axis 4 modulates rather than decides: it sets what a paper is allowed to be reported as saying.

## 7. Coding discipline and failure modes

**Code from the paper, not its reputation.**
Read the passage carrying the claim.
A verdict formed from the abstract is `[C]`, not `[V]`.

**Record evidence for each code** — one sentence and a locator per axis.
A code without evidence cannot be contested, and an uncontestable code is not a finding.

**Falsification hook per programme verdict.**
What specific result, or what specific response to a specific anomaly, would change it.
If nothing could, it is a stance rather than an assessment and should be reported as one.

**Symmetry check.**
After coding, take the programme the review is most sympathetic to and re-code it as if arguing against it.
If it survives with all four axes clean, either it genuinely is in good shape — which happens — or the grid was applied with different force in different directions.
Report which conclusion was reached and why.

**Field knowledge is evidence.**
Practitioner testimony, professional experience, and knowledge produced outside dominant academic centres bear directly on axis 3, where the question is what actors actually do, and on axis 2, where it is how a community actually behaves.
This material is under-indexed by every citation database and will not arrive through any cascade; it must be sought deliberately, and its provenance stated as explicitly as any journal citation.

**The grid's own limits.**
It was built to catch one failure: a theory applied to a domain, insulated from correction, confirmed by the practice it produced.
It is not sensitive to fraud, to statistical malpractice inside an otherwise progressive programme, to publication bias, or to a field whose whole question is badly posed.
Where one of those is the live risk, say so rather than reporting a clean grid as a clean bill of health.
