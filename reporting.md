# Reporting

Templates for the deliverables, plus the obstacle taxonomy for the epistemological section. Adapt the wording; keep the sections — each exists because its absence is what makes a review unauditable.

## Programme dossier

```markdown
# Programme: <name>

**Hard core** — the propositions the programme does not give up.

**Central works** — with identifiers.

**Axis 2 — dynamics: progressive | degenerating | indeterminate**
Evidence: stance distribution over the forward channel (counts of extending /
rescuing / critical-empirical / affirming-applies), then two or three anomalies
traced through the citing literature with what was done with each.
Falsification hook.

**Axis 3 — contamination: contaminated | independent | indeterminate**
Diffusion channel, with evidence. Feedback path into the tested quantity, with
evidence. Approximate diffusion date. Direction (Barnesian / counterperformative).
Works published before diffusion, listed — these carry uncontaminated evidence.
Falsification hook.

**Inheritance** — papers assigned here, and every override with its reason.
```

## Review document

```markdown
# <Title>

## Question and scope
Theses as propositions. Scope boundary. What the corpus structurally cannot
contain, and why.

## Method
Rounds run, key-set versions and their movement, the three channels and their
declared completeness regimes, sources per discipline, manual compensation
performed, tier counts, budget. Point to the search log and the ledger.

## Corpus map
Programmes identified, their weight in the corpus, axis 2 and 3 verdicts in a
compact table. Result of the path-dependence test, if run.

## Synthesis
Organised by thesis — not by paper, not chronologically. Per thesis: what is
claimed, by which programme, produced by which evidence modes, with what
purchase on the claim, and with what reservations from axes 1-3.

State the warrant explicitly. "Reported as a robust regularity, but every
confirmation postdates the theory's diffusion into practice" is a usable
finding. "X has been shown" usually is not.

## What the corpus has not settled
Open disputes, indeterminate verdicts, questions no programme in the corpus asks.

## What the corpus cannot settle
See the taxonomy below. Obligatory section.

## Adversarial pass
The strongest published objection to this review's own central conclusion,
stated at its strongest, with the response. If none was found, say so, and say
whether that is a fact about the debate or about the corpus boundary.

## Exclusion and demotion registers

## Verification status
Claims marked [V] were checked against the passage in the source. [C] were not.
Both appear in the text.
```

## The "cannot settle" taxonomy

For each thesis, ask two questions: **what kind of knowledge would settle this**, and **is that kind producible on this object**. Where it is not, the thesis is not merely unsettled — it is unsettleable in the current state, which is a positive finding and should be reported as one rather than as an admission.

Most of the material is already in hand from the other axes:

| Obstacle | What it means | Where the evidence comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Performativity | Confirmation cannot be independent of the theory | Axis 3, already instructed |
| Value-laden categories | The dispute is about what to count, not about counts | Axis 1, already instructed |
| Reflexivity of the object | The object responds to being studied or described | Axis 3, plus the nature of the field |
| Historical singularity | One realisation, no counterfactual case available | Scope and object |
| Non-ergodicity | The process does not repeat under stable conditions, so sampling over time is not sampling from a fixed population | Object |
| Access closure | The decisive material is confidential, destroyed, or held by parties with an interest | Search log |
| Measurement impossibility | The quantity in the thesis has no operationalisation that survives the concept | Axis 4 purchase codings |
| Mode unavailability | The only mode with purchase on this claim type cannot be applied here — e.g. intervention is impossible or unethical | Axis 4 |

Distinguish *unsettleable in principle* from *unsettleable given current access, instruments or institutions*. The second is contingent and worth stating as a research agenda: name what would have to change.

Where a thesis is unsettleable, say what can be known instead — usually a weaker claim at a different claim type, which is a real result and not a consolation prize.

## Formatting conventions

- Compact tables. Identifiers in the ledger, not scattered through prose.
- Developed prose for reasoning; bullets only for genuine enumerations.
- `[V]` / `[C]` inline on substantive claims.
- Figures, exact citations and dates unqualified only if verified; otherwise `[to verify]`.
- Quotations under fifteen words, attributed, rare — paraphrase carries the argument.
- Date by project phase or retrieval date, not by implied permanence. The forward channel has a shelf life.

## Handover note

Close with what a re-run needs: the key-set version and its derivation criteria, queries and vocabularies, retrieval dates, tier thresholds, and which programme verdicts are likeliest to have moved. A review that cannot be re-run is a snapshot presented as a map.
